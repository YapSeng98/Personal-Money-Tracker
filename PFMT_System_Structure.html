Rules the structure encodes

  • No exchange rate exists anywhere. Currency is a plain string on each row; amounts in different currencies are never added. Every total is computed per currency.
  • A movement between accounts is two rows, not one. A transfer writes an expense leg and an income leg sharing a transfer_group, so both balances move through the ordinary logic. Cross-currency legs keep their own amounts — S$1,000 out and RM3,173.30 in — with no rate invented between them.
  • The group prefix records what kind of pair it is: tg_ a transfer, ag_ an asset purchase. Reading the prefix rather than the peer's type means the pair survives even if a type fails to round-trip.
  • Neither leg is earning or spending. Analytics excludes paired rows from income and expense; a cross-currency pair is reported separately as exchanged in / out.
  • A payback reduces spending rather than adding income — money returned by someone else was never earnings.
  • Balance is derived, not stored as truth. current_balance is the opening figure; the live balance is that plus income and assets, less expenses, counting only rows whose currency matches the account's.
